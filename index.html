About me

I am a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Jiannong Cao, and a member of the Internet and Mobile Computing Lab (IMCL). From November 2025 to June 2026, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Bologna, working with Prof. Paolo Bellavista. I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from South China University of Technology in 2021, advised by Prof. Lei Yang.

My current research focuses on on-device large language models (LLMs), particularly optimizing LLM inference and agent execution on mobile devices. My previous research explored container virtualization and edge computing.
